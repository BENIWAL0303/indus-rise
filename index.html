01 — WHO WE ARE

Big ideas begin with curious minds.

We bring together students who want to explore entrepreneurship, turn ideas into action, and build things that matter.

Whether you want to launch a tech venture, design innovative consumer goods, or discover how early-stage ventures operate, Indus Rise DTC provides the community, resources, and peer network to accelerate your learning curve.

04 — VISION & MISSION

Empowering the next generation.

Building a resilient bridge between academic learning and high-impact entrepreneurial endeavors.

OUR VISION

To build the most vibrant, founder-friendly campus community in the region.

We envision an ecosystem where every student feels empowered to identify pressing societal and technological challenges, formulate bold solutions, and take the fearless leap from learner to founder.

OUR MISSION

Actionable toolkits, founder mentorship, and peer collaboration.

Our mission is to democratize entrepreneurship across engineering and business streams. We provide the mentorship, physical sandbox, and investor exposure needed to transform dorm-room ideas into scalable ventures.
