OUR ORIGIN & VALUES

Cultivating the next generation of collegiate founders.

Indus Rise DTC was founded by second-year student builders determined to bridge the gap between classroom theory and real venture creation.

01 — HOW IT STARTED

Built by students, for students.

Traditional campus clubs often focus heavily on guest lectures and theoretical discussions. We wanted something radically different: a builder-first playground where students can fail safely, test relentlessly, and launch confidently.

We recognized that students possess immense technical ingenuity, artistic flair, and problem-solving passion, but frequently lack the co-founder network, mentor guidance, and investor access to turn side projects into sustainable business models.

Indus Rise DTC is that missing catalyst. We operate like a decentralized early-stage incubator right in our campus ecosystem.

02 — CORE TENETS

The principles that guide our work.

Every workshop, pitch competition, and sprint we organize is rooted in these four non-negotiable community values.

01. Radical Curiosity

We encourage questioning conventional solutions. Breakthrough companies happen when students inspect daily frictions and ask: "Why hasn't anyone fixed this yet?"

02. Bias for Action

A rough working prototype in the hands of three real customers is infinitely more valuable than a 40-page theoretical business deck. We build, ship, and measure fast.

03. Cross-Disciplinary Synergy

Engineers need storytellers. Business thinkers need hackers. Designers need product operators. We create deliberate intersections where diverse minds co-create.

04. Real-World Truth

We discard fabricated vanity metrics. We respect genuine customer feedback, real market traction, and authentic founder grit above superficial hype.

03 — OUR JOURNEY

Milestones that shaped Indus Rise.

How a student-led initiative evolved into our campus's most recognized entrepreneurship cell.

Late 2024

The Dorm Room Spark

A cohort of second-year engineering students gathered to discuss why students with brilliant coding and design skills weren't building startups. The initial blueprint of Indus Rise was drafted.

Early 2025

Official Collegiate Charter

Indus Rise received official faculty and administrative sponsorship, establishing our Innovation Cell and onboarding our inaugural cohort of 60 founding members.

2026 (Current)

Incubation Sprints & Pitch Tank '26

Expanded to 150+ members across 4 engineering and management departments. Launched regular weekend MVP build hackathons and initiated partnerships with regional angel investors.

2027 Vision

Inter-Collegiate Startup Alliance

Establishing student venture syndicates, providing micro-grant funding for campus prototypes, and launching an annual regional collegiate entrepreneurship summit.
