OUR ACTIONABLE FRAMEWORK

From ideas to action. Three core pillars.

We take students through a structured journey from finding meaningful problem spaces to launching functional prototypes and securing early-stage incubation.

PILLAR 01

Ideation & Problem Discovery

Great companies are rarely born from isolated brainstorms; they emerge from deep empathy with user frustrations. We teach students how to unearth real market needs before writing a single line of code.

Customer Interview Drills

Learn Mom Test techniques to interview target users without asking leading questions, uncovering their actual workflow bottlenecks.

Reverse Pitching Sessions

Industry partners and alumni present real operational challenges from logistics, healthcare, and education for student teams to solve.

Lean Canvas Workshops

Condense a 30-page corporate business plan into a living 1-page canvas outlining value propositions, channels, and unfair advantages.

PILLAR 02

Innovation & Rapid Prototyping

We move past wireframes and mockups into live, clickable, production-grade MVPs. With modern frameworks, AI APIs, and no-code tools, student teams can ship software in days instead of months.

48-Hour MVP Hackathons

Intense weekend sprint where engineers, designers, and marketers lock in to launch deployed products evaluated by external judges.

Tech Stack & Cloud Credits

Access sponsored cloud credits, AI model tokens, and database infrastructure to host, test, and benchmark prototypes at zero cost.

Code & UI Reviews

Peer-to-peer code reviews, security audits, and UI design critiques by senior student engineers and industry technical mentors.

PILLAR 03

Growth, Incubation & Pitching

Building an MVP is only half the battle. We prepare teams to present their traction, negotiate term sheets, access university incubation resources, and pitch to angel syndicates.

Indus Pitch Tank

Our premier flagship pitch event where shortlisted campus startups pitch live on stage in front of active venture fund partners.

Incubation Backing

Direct coordination with our institutional Innovation & Incubation Cell for workspace grants, legal entity incorporation, and IP patent filing.

Founder Office Hours

1-on-1 advisory sessions with serial entrepreneurs who provide candid critique on go-to-market strategies and pitch decks.

THE FOUNDER JOURNEY

The 4-stage acceleration path.

How any student at our college can walk in with curiosity and graduate with a live company.

STAGE 01

Discovery

Team formation, identifying high-impact customer problems, validating willingness to pay.

STAGE 02

Prototyping

Building MVP software or hardware demo, implementing user auth, running closed alpha tests.

STAGE 03

Validation

Acquiring your first 50 organic users, tracking retention metrics, refining user experience.

STAGE 04

Rise & Scale

Pitch Tank presentation, applying for seed grants, entering national accelerator cohorts.
