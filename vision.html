OUR NORTH STAR & MANIFESTO

Empowering the next generation of disruptors.

Building a permanent, resilient bridge between collegiate academic excellence and high-impact entrepreneurial endeavors.

OUR VISION 2030

To build the most vibrant, founder-friendly campus community in the region.

We envision an ecosystem where every student—regardless of whether they are studying computer science, electronics, business, or civil engineering—feels inherently equipped to identify pressing societal and technological challenges, formulate bold solutions, and take the fearless leap from learner to founder.

By 2030, our goal is to incubate 50+ student ventures with institutional backing and over ₹1 Crore in external capital raised by student alumni.

OUR MISSION

Actionable toolkits, founder mentorship, and peer collaboration.

Our mission is to democratize entrepreneurship across engineering and business streams. We provide the mentorship, physical sandbox, and investor exposure needed to transform dorm-room ideas into scalable ventures.

We replace passive classroom webinars with tactile sprints, live customer validation, and competitive pitch stages where students own their outcomes.

COMMUNITY PLEDGE

The 5 commitments of Indus Rise DTC.

What every member, mentor, and student founder can hold our leadership team accountable to.

1. Zero Gatekeeping

Access to our events, hackathons, and founder toolkits is open to all students across all academic years without prerequisites or elite filtering.

2. Bias for Honest Validation

We celebrate failure when it leads to authentic learning. We help founders listen to hard market feedback rather than encouraging echo chambers.

3. Direct Founder-to-Founder Matching

We actively bridge technical engineers with operational and design minds to form balanced, complimentary founding teams.

4. Institutional Incubation Pathways

We provide seamless pipelines into college innovation cells, government student startup grants, and patent facilitation.

5. Lifelong Alumni Network

Our relationship does not end at graduation. We nurture a strong, active alumni network of founders and operators who mentor younger cohorts.
